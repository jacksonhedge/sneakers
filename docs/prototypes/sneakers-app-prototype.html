Tab 1 · Agent

Cover-flow of models, one equipped

iOS-6-album-style carousel: swipe (or tap a side orb) to browse your models — each is a colored orb. The equipped one breathes and trades; tap Equip to switch. Unsubscribed models show their price right in the carousel.

  • Paper-live in v1 — real prices, simulated balance; the agent runs 24/7 on a Railway worker
  • Only one model equipped at a time; Equip is instant, next window uses the new model
  • Activity feed carries the venue logo per trade — Kalshi, Polymarket, ProphetX for sports
  • Tap the centered orb for its detail sheet

Tab 2 · Models

My Model vs Trading Agents

My Model is yours to train: the strategy prompt writes agent config the worker re-reads each window. Trading Agents is a ranked, subscribable marketplace — tap + to add one, or open it for details.

  • Prompt + presets wired to PUT /api/agent/config
  • Trading Agents ranked by 30-day paper performance; subscriptions bill via Stripe, creator payouts via Stripe Connect
  • v1: Sneakers Labs models subscribable first; community submissions in review

Tab 3 · Funds

Real Stripe rails, test keys

Add cash runs a genuine Payment Element flow in test mode and feeds the agent's paper balance. Flip to live keys when custody and compliance are settled — the UI doesn't change.

  • Balance + ledger from the server, single source of truth with the agent
  • Try it: Add cash actually updates the balance here

Tab 4 · Profile

You, your plan, your connections

Account at the top, then everything the agent plugs into: your Sneakers plan (Stripe Billing), trading venues, and data connections. Kalshi and Polymarket connect for real in v1; the rest appear as cards so the full vision is visible at launch.

  • Profile + plan management up top; sign out at the bottom
  • ProphetX + OddsJam data plan listed as available / coming soon

Tap the tab bar to move around · Pause the agent · Add cash on Funds. Open this page on your iPhone to feel it at real size.
